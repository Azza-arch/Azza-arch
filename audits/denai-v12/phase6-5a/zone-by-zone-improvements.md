# A–G decisions

**KEEP** preserves a successful feature. **MODIFY** changes an existing feature. **REMOVE** removes a specific obstruction, not the mission. **PROPOSE** requires design approval and implementation validation. Complexity: S = local presentation work; M = interaction/state integration; L = several coupled systems and regression. Estimates are relative, not delivery commitments.

## A — start, crate, first gap · x0–1458

- **Current / physical:** run, jump, push a real dynamic crate onto the pressure plate; optional ledge reward. No required math popup. The plate visibly changes access: a good action/consequence introduction.
- **Visual:** large sky and dirt fields; huge rectangular tree canopy; repeated dark vertical dirt marks resemble joints or walls. [A evidence](../../level-v12/phase6/visit/screenshots/natural-a-start.png).
- **KEEP** crate, plate, easy jumps, checkpoint and landmark tree. **MODIFY** duplicate UI messages and interior dirt seams. **PROPOSE** sparse plant groups near the start, never on the gap lip. Do not shrink or rebuild the tree just for novelty.
- **Confirmed assets:** crate9, plate148/149, deck48–50, plant124, existing canopy/trunk helper, flag111/112.
- **Risks / complexity:** visual S; no crate body or reward condition change. A decorative trunk must not imply a wall on the walking plane. Retest crate/ledge if art moves near them.
