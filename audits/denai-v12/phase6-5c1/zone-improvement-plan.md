# Zone improvement plan

Each zone's assigned role (from the brief §4) plus whether it already reads that way, what's missing, and what to change — grounded in visual-audit.md's findings, not invented.

## A — "readable beginning and environmental introduction"

**Current state**: reads correctly as a beginning (one tree landmark, simple crate puzzle, clear forward path) but the ~650px `A-crate` stretch is sparse and the sky is flat the whole way.
**Change**: depth-layer-plan.md's background band (every zone gets it); 1–2 more tile-124 accents in the 650–1370 gap (reuse, no new frame). No identity-specific prop needed — "introduction" role is already served by pacing/simplicity, not scenery density.

## B — "lift and control bank as focal point"

**Current state**: the lift/control bank cluster (posts, beam, actuator-mark row, lever, selector) already reads as the focal point — this is correctly built. The approach to it (B-bank→B-terrace→B-overlook, 324px) is visually flat.
**Change**: fill the approach with existing tile-124 bushes (same frame as A/E) so the walk *to* the focal point isn't a bare staircase; don't add anything to the lift cluster itself — it doesn't need help.

## C — "elevated optional discovery path"

**Current state**: already deliberately minimal per its own phase report ("no façade or café objects") — correctly distinct from the main route by being *elevated and sparse*, which itself is the identity (an optional side-path should feel different, i.e. quieter, not busier).
**Change**: none required for identity. Only the sign/post near-overlap noted in coordinate-issues.md is worth a minor nudge (not a new prop).

## D — "recognizable number-block workshop"

**Current state**: has the number-block mechanic itself (visually distinct machine) but the approach (D-approach, first 300px) has nothing marking it as a "workshop" versus generic ground, and the two signs at x=3372/3420 risk visually merging (coordinate-issues.md).
**Change**: this is the one zone where the brief's role ("workshop") isn't yet visually supported by anything beyond the number-block tiles themselves. Cheapest fix within "no new fabricated structures": the existing `D-rack` oneWay platform (x=3078, already real geometry) could host a few more of the already-used block/rack-style atlas frames to read as shelving, since the rack *is* real workshop-shaped geometry already, just undecorated. This is reusing existing frames on existing geometry, not inventing a structure.

## E — "coherent pipe and waterworks environment"

**Current state**: confirmed live via screenshots — the pipe run, source tank, reservoir, and control signs are all present and functionally coherent, but visually the long straight pipe run (10 identical segments) reads as repetitive, and the pipe-as-walkway (confirmed by direct play) isn't visually marked as different from non-walkable pipe segments.
**Change**: P1 — vary which pipe frame renders at the walkway segment (e.g. a distinct cap/joint frame already in the atlas, not a new asset) so the walkable stretch is visually legible as a path rather than indistinguishable decoration. This is the only zone where a *functional* read (what can I walk on) is at stake, not just aesthetics — worth prioritizing above pure decoration elsewhere.

## F — "interesting hill traversal and platforming rhythm"

**Current state**: the ascent (F-entry→step1→step2) already has a bush at every step and genuinely reads as a hill. The descent (F-down1→down2→river-view) has the same decoration density but a perfectly uniform diagonal shelf pattern, reading as a staircase rather than a hillside.
**Change**: P1 — no coordinate/collision change; vary shelf width perception by alternating which existing atlas frame decorates each descent shelf (e.g. not every shelf gets the same bush frame), and/or let one shelf's prop sit asymmetrically (not centered) to break the uniform-staircase read. Cave + moving platform sub-area already has its own distinct identity; untouched.

## G — "memorable river and destination finale"

**Current state**: already the most bespoke zone in the game (custom split-hinge lever texture, dedicated river-fill loop, door+flag). This role is already fulfilled.
**Change**: P2 only — the deep repeating water-tile fill below the crossing (coordinate-issues.md) is low-priority since it's outside normal camera framing during play. No change required to meet the "memorable finale" bar; it already clears it.

## Net assessment

Five of seven zones (A, B, C, F, G) already substantially fulfill their assigned role; the gap is cross-zone pacing/density (solved once by the depth-layer-plan background) plus a few local decoration tweaks (P1/P2). D is the only zone where the *role itself* isn't yet visually supported and needs a small, geometry-reusing nudge. E is the only zone with a borderline functional-legibility issue (walkable pipe not visually marked), which should be treated as higher priority than pure decoration work elsewhere.
