# Lorong Atas Bumbung — human playtest checklist

Run with a new player who has not seen the controls or puzzle solutions. Ask them to think aloud, but do not teach unless they are fully stuck; record when help was needed. Obtain consent before recording audio or video. Do not lead the player toward a preferred answer.

## Observe and ask

1. Can the player find how to start? What do they expect each title-screen option to do?
2. Can they learn movement and jumping from the brief in-game hints without reading a long tutorial?
3. Do they understand the first crate puzzle and what changed when it worked?
4. Do they understand why a function input controls a mechanism? What do they think the output represents?
5. Is the waterworks puzzle understandable? Can they explain why the function order matters?
6. Do they notice either optional route? Do they understand how to return from it?
7. Does the final bridge feel rewarding? What made it feel that way, or not?
8. Where do they hesitate, repeat an action, guess, or ask for help? Record the location and what they tried.
9. How long do they take in this session, and how much help did they receive?
10. Would they replay or share the game? Why?
