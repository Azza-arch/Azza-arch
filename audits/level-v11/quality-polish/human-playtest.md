# Human playtest: questions and limits

## Questions for the next beta session

1. Without hints, did you understand how to activate A's crate/plate and why the bonus platform appears?
2. Did the B lift read as a moving route while it was parked? Did you understand the short wait after solving?
3. Did you notice C as an optional route? If you visited it, were the mapping task and return ladder obvious?
4. Were D's number blocks and formula easy to associate? Did an incorrect answer make the next attempt clear?
5. Did E's water-flow animation feel connected to your answer? Was its duration satisfying or did you want to move sooner?
6. Did F's open ridge and cave roof make the optional cave easy to notice? Could you leave and rejoin the main route without guidance?
7. At G, did the lever visibly cause the bridge to move? Did the handle look attached to the hinge and did the bridge feel safe only after it settled?
8. On a real phone, did the player/mechanism stay visible around the touch controls and math panel?
9. Where did you stop, hesitate, or retry? Which prompt or visual did you expect to behave as a platform?

## Limits

- Natural-input scripts use keyboard/touch events in Chromium and deterministic coordinate waits. Their 89.39 / 82.70 second durations are automation times, not player completion-time estimates or evidence of enjoyment/difficulty.
- No human participant has tried this V11.2 build yet. Automated bot traversal cannot establish perceived difficulty, pacing, route discovery or whether the visual changes feel better.
- Mobile evidence is emulation only. Test current Safari/Firefox, real iOS/Android devices, audio, keyboard/screen-reader behavior and save behavior across sessions before production review.
