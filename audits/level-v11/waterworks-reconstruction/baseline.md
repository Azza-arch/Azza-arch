# V11.2 Zone E baseline and reference

The supplied V11.2 image was inspected as a composition guide. It shows a natural left terrace, a low supported F/G pipe rack, a supported output reservoir and lever, the crossing, and a lower cave route. It is not used as a source for available sprites. The locked 180-frame catalog remains the source of sprite identity.

The pre-change V11.1 browser capture is `screenshots/zone-e-before-locked-detail.png`. It shows a high, long horizontal pipe, tall isolated posts, a tank-like water output and world text over the mechanism. The current implementation was captured at matching camera scale in `screenshots/zone-e-after-entry-canvas.png` and the full page in `screenshots/zone-e-after-entry-full.png`.

The baseline screenshot was captured before source edits from the local V11.1 game. The reconstruction screenshots are captured from the modified V11 code. V9 and V10 are not used as layout substitutes.
