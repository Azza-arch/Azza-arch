# Science Quest — license audit

## Evidence inspected

The ZIP contains one license file: `License.txt`. Its text identifies “UI Pack (2.0)”, credits Kenney, gives a creation date of 12-06-2024, and states **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**. The included text says the content is free for personal, educational, and commercial projects and that crediting Kenney or `kenney.nl` is not mandatory.

The bundled `Preview.png` and `Sample.png` also visibly contain a CC0 mark and a short commercial-use statement, consistent with `License.txt`.

## Finding

For the contents represented as part of this Kenney UI Pack, the supplied documentation explicitly permits personal, educational, and commercial use without required attribution. Attribution is recommended as a courtesy, not stated as a condition.

## Limits and follow-up

- The archive has no separate license/readme files per asset or per file type. The finding is based on the pack-wide `License.txt`; no contradictory included terms were found.
- The two bundled TTF fonts and six OGG sound files are within the same supplied pack and no separate license for them is included. The pack license appears to cover the pack, but preserve this evidence and re-check the source page if distributing those files separately or if provenance is challenged.
- The archive includes URL shortcuts to Kenney and Patreon, but no source-page snapshot or checksum/signature to independently prove the archive’s provenance.
- If legal certainty is required for a commercial release, retain the original ZIP and license, and verify the current license/source with Kenney. This is a documentation-based audit, not legal advice.

**Status:** documented permission is clear for use of this pack in commercial projects; no mandatory attribution is stated. Separate provenance verification is not performed.
