Good to know.

How does a project usually work?

You send the basics by email or WhatsApp. I reply with questions and a written quotation based on scope. Once you accept, I build the site, you review it and ask for changes within the agreed revision rounds, then I help you launch. Timing depends on scope and on how quickly your content is ready, so I confirm it in the quotation.

What do I need to prepare?

What the business does, who your customers are, the one action you want visitors to take (for example WhatsApp you), plus your logo, photos and price or service details if you have them. Not sure yet? Send what you have and we can work out the rest.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Landing pages, sales pages, portfolios and small business websites are the core of what I do. I am also open to custom web systems such as bookings or dashboards, but those are quoted individually after a scoping conversation and are not part of the website packages.

What if I only need something very simple?

Try KadMeHQ first. You can start free, and it may already be enough for a simple digital presence.

How do I contact you?

Email is best for project details, so scope and decisions stay written down. For a quick question, WhatsApp is fine. Boleh berbincang dalam Bahasa Melayu atau English.

Are there yearly costs?

Usually yes, but they go to other companies, not to me. A domain is renewed every year at the registrar's price, and hosting depends on how your site is built. A simple static site may fit a free hosting tier, but free tiers can change, so I do not promise free hosting forever. I confirm the actual third-party cost before you buy.

Is the care plan mandatory?

No. You can look after your own domain, hosting and content updates. The optional care plan for static sites is RM300 per year: a quarterly check and up to four small content updates a year, each taking up to 30 minutes. Unused updates do not carry over. WordPress and dynamic systems need a separately scoped agreement.

Who owns my domain?

You do. I register or transfer the domain in your name where practical, and hand over the hosting access, the site files and a short note on what renews and when. I do not keep a customer's domain under my personal ownership.

How do payment and quotations work?

You get a written quotation valid for 14 days. Payment is 50% as a development deposit to start, and 50% when you accept the agreed site, before it goes live. Domain and hosting are separate from the development fee unless your quotation says otherwise.

Are changes after launch included?

For 30 days after launch I fix bugs within the agreed original scope. A bug is something in the agreed work that does not behave as agreed, such as a broken link or a layout fault on a supported device. A new request is a change to what was agreed, such as a new page, feature, redesign, or new text. New requests are quoted separately, or use a care-plan update if you have one.

What happens if I do not renew?

If a required domain or hosting service expires, the website can stop loading. Email or other services connected to the domain may also be affected until the renewal is fixed. The handover note lists each renewal so it is easy to track.

Can I use my own hosting?

Yes. I can deploy to suitable hosting you already have, help you arrange hosting, or use suitable static hosting when it fits the project.
